Standby is a private screening room that lives in your browser. Play a file, paste a direct link, share your screen or a YouTube tab — the stream travels straight from your machine to your friends, frame for frame.

  • Local testing just works. Tabs in the same browser talk over a built-in channel — no server, no internet, nothing to install. Cross-device rooms use a signalling broker.
  • Everyone waits at the door. You approve each guest by name — and can show anyone out, or cut anyone's share, instantly.
  • Full quality, no relay. Media moves peer-to-peer with resolution locked and headroom up to ~14 Mb/s.

Watch-together mode

Streaming sites like YouTube never expose their raw video, so it can't be piped into the room's player — that's a hard platform limit, not a bug. The supported way: we open the video in its own clean player tab and share that tab — picture and audio go to everyone live.

  1. Click START — a player tab opens.
  2. Press play there.
  3. In the share picker choose that tab and tick “Share tab audio”.
  4. Keep the tab visible while watching. Stop anytime with the stage's STOP button.
